# Getting started

Power on the device and check that it started correctly.

## Before you start

- The device is wired to a 5 V or 12 V supply.
- An SD card containing the screen images is inserted. See [SD card, themes and settings file](../advanced/sd-card-and-themes.md).

## Steps

1. Switch on the supply.
2. A start-up (splash) picture appears for about 3 seconds.
3. The **main screen** appears: six large buttons **IN 1** to **IN 6** and a blue **Settings** button along the bottom.

## What you should see

- **IN 1** has a green frame. The device always starts with all relays off, which is input IN 1. It does not remember the input you used last.
- The screen brightness is the level you saved last time (100 % the first time).

## Things to know

- If the SD card is missing or unreadable, the device still works, but the buttons are plain grey rectangles with no labels. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
- The default button labels are IN 1 VHF-AM1, IN 2 VHF-AM2, IN 3 VOR/ILS/GS, IN 4 MARKER, IN 5 DME and IN 6 AUX. These are part of the picture on the SD card, so your device may show others.
